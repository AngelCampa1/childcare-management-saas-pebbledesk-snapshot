## The problem: Compliance issues often start with messy child and guardian records

If children, guardians, classrooms, pickup permissions, and billing contacts do not stay aligned, every downstream workflow gets noisier. PebbleDesk treats records as operating infrastructure, not background admin.

Teams moving from paper can start with the [free childcare enrollment forms and agreement template](/free/childcare-enrollment-agreement-template), then turn the signed registration, authorized pickup, emergency contact, health, and tuition details into cleaner digital records.

## The PebbleDesk solution: Use one structured enrollment record across daily workflows

The product supports children, guardian records, classrooms, authorized pickup, billing contacts, and subsidy-related record structure so directors can manage the center from one system. That makes attendance, billing, and communication more credible because the foundation is cleaner.

## What to review next

Go to Attendance Tracking if your main issue is daily execution. Go to Billing & Payments if the records problem shows up first in invoices, balances, or subsidy follow-up.

## Why record structure affects compliance

Enrollment records are not only a roster. They determine which guardians can be contacted, who is authorized for pickup, what classroom a child belongs to, which billing contact receives invoices, and whether subsidy details are available when attendance is reviewed. When those fields live in different places, staff end up solving the same record problem repeatedly.

PebbleDesk brings that foundation closer to the workflows that depend on it. A clean child and guardian record makes daily attendance easier, gives billing follow-up a clearer owner, and helps directors respond faster when a licensing or subsidy question points back to enrollment documentation.

## Accuracy note

This page focuses on PebbleDesk's enrollment, guardian, classroom, billing-contact, and import workflows. It does not claim to replace every document-management process a center may use. The practical goal is to reduce scattered spreadsheets and legacy roster exports while keeping child and family records easier to review.

Enrollment is the quiet foundation for almost every other feature. If guardian contacts, pickup permissions, subsidy context, classroom placement, and dates are wrong, the downstream workflows become harder no matter how polished the screen looks. PebbleDesk connects enrollment to [attendance tracking](/features/attendance-tracking), [billing and payments](/features/billing-payments), and the [parent portal](/features/parent-portal) so directors are not maintaining separate versions of the same child record.
