## The problem: Attendance becomes rework when it is separated from the record

Directors do not just need to know that a child arrived. They need the record to hold up later when a guardian questions a charge, a subsidy case needs support, or a licensing review starts. That means attendance has to stay connected to the rest of the operating workflow.

PebbleDesk keeps attendance inside the same system as classrooms, ratios, billing, and reports. The point is not a flashy kiosk story. The point is a record that still makes sense at the end of the day.

## The PebbleDesk solution: Keep attendance tied to rooms, ratios, and follow-up

Attendance in PebbleDesk supports digital capture, room-aware daily history, and reporting that can be pulled into downstream finance and compliance work. It is useful on its own, but it becomes much more valuable because it feeds the rest of the operating record.

That matters most for centers that are tired of exporting a roster from one tool, checking room status in another, and cleaning up documentation manually before every claim review or inspection.

## What the workflow covers

If attendance is the starting point for your record problem, review ratio tracking and audit reports next. If the bigger issue is that your child, guardian, and classroom data is fragmented before the first sign-in even happens, go to Enrollment & Records first.

## Where attendance fits next

A director reviewing attendance should be able to answer more than who tapped in. The useful question is whether the record can support the rest of the day: which classroom the child was in, whether staffing matched the room count, and whether billing or subsidy follow-up has the right dates. The front office is not rebuilding yesterday from sticky notes, classroom texts, and a spreadsheet before a family question or licensing request.
