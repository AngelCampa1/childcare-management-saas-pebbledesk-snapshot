## The problem: Multi-site owners need visibility without pretending every center is the same

PebbleDesk is strongest when it starts with the compliance problem directors feel every day, but that does not mean the product stops at one center. Multi-site programs need cross-center visibility described carefully, with practical oversight instead of inflated enterprise promises.

## The PebbleDesk solution: Give operators a clearer view across locations and workflows

Multi-site support is real, and larger groups still deserve a careful rollout conversation. PebbleDesk gives operators cross-center visibility while keeping setup, migration, and reporting expectations clear before a full launch.

## What to review next

If you are still working out how live data gets into the system, review Imports & Migration next. If you need to understand how cross-center reporting connects to audit work, go to Audit Reports.

## Accuracy note

This page keeps multi-location language qualified because rollout depends on plan fit, data migration, and support needs. PebbleDesk supports center switching and cross-center visibility for group-oriented setups, while larger operators should confirm packaging and rollout details before planning a full migration.

## What directors should review next

Multi-location oversight should help owners compare centers without flattening important local differences. A licensed childcare group may need to look across attendance, ratios, billing, and audit readiness, but each location still has its own rooms, staff, families, and state expectations. PebbleDesk connects this page to [audit reports](/features/audit-reports), [staff scheduling](/features/staff-scheduling), and imports so the rollout story stays grounded in real operations. The product is not being positioned as a complex enterprise command center. The better promise is practical visibility for owner/operators who need to know where follow-up is needed before a small issue becomes a licensing or cash-flow problem. Directors at each site still manage the day. Owners get a clearer view of patterns, gaps, and work that should not be hidden in separate local spreadsheets.

That context helps during rollout conversations too. A group can check which records each center already keeps clean, then use [imports and migration](/features/imports-migration) planning to bring the messy pieces over without losing local context.
