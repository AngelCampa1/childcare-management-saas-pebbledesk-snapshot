## The problem: Billing gets messy when finance is detached from enrollment

Many childcare teams end up with a finance workflow that barely talks to enrollment or attendance. PebbleDesk is more useful when billing lives inside the same operating system, even if your center still uses a separate accounting stack beyond it.

## The PebbleDesk solution: Keep invoices and payment status close to the family record

PebbleDesk supports invoices, payment status, public guardian payment links, and the account context a center needs to manage billing clearly. {{claim.quickBooksSupport}} Directors should confirm the setup path that fits their plan and accounting workflow.

## What this page covers

If your main problem is subsidy-heavy month-end work, go to Subsidy Billing. If you need help getting existing finance and family data into the system first, go to Imports & Migration.

## What to review next

The day-to-day billing problem is rarely just creating an invoice. Directors need to know whether the child record, guardian account, subsidy context, payment link, and current balance all tell the same story. PebbleDesk keeps the finance workflow close to [enrollment records](/features/enrollment-records), [subsidy billing](/features/subsidy-billing), and attendance history so the office is not copying the same family details into another tool. That also makes exceptions easier to review. A late pickup fee, tuition change, or public payment link should not become a separate shadow record that only one person understands. PebbleDesk can support centers that still keep a separate accounting process, including qualified QuickBooks-supported setups, while keeping the director-side operating record clear. The goal is practical: fewer stale invoices, cleaner family conversations, and a billing trail that still makes sense when someone asks why a charge exists. During rollout, directors should also decide who reviews balance changes and how families are told when a charge is corrected. PebbleDesk keeps that conversation anchored in the current invoice record, which is easier to manage than emailing a separate spreadsheet or asking staff to remember which copy is final.
