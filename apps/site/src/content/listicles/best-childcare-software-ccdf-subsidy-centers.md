## Why CCDF billing breaks most childcare software

CCDF is a federal subsidy program administered at the state level, which means every state has its own billing submission format, authorization tracking requirements, and audit documentation standards. Software built for parent communication handles enrollment, daily reports, and family messaging well. It does not handle the reconciliation between attendance records and billing claims that CCDF audits require.

The core problem: most childcare platforms store attendance in one place and billing in another. When those two systems don't talk to each other, reconciliation is manual. Manual reconciliation means errors. Federal data attributes 40% of CCDF improper payment findings to missing or insufficient documentation, not fraud, not intentional overbilling, documentation gaps.

### What CCDF-ready software looks like

A platform earns consideration for CCDF-heavy programs if it does four things:

1. **Attendance records that create billing evidence automatically**: check-in and check-out timestamps that are tamper-evident and tie to specific children, authorizations, and billing periods
2. **Authorization tracking**: expired CCDF authorizations are one of the fastest paths to a billing finding; software should flag when an authorization is approaching expiration before you submit a claim
3. **Attendance-to-claim reconciliation**: the billing submission should be derivable from attendance records without manual matching
4. **Audit trail in state licensing officer format**, not a parent-readable summary, but a documented record that satisfies a state auditor reviewing your program

The platforms that check these boxes are listed first in this evaluation.

### The provider participation collapse

53% of CCDF providers have left the subsidy system since 2006. HHS OIG research found that 34% of licensed providers who don't currently serve CCDF families say they would refuse even if asked. Administrative burden is a documented reason.

That burden is partly unavoidable; federal and state program requirements are real. But the portion that comes from running attendance tracking and billing in separate systems, or from spending 8-12 hours monthly on manual reconciliation, is addressable with the right software.

### Evaluating platforms for your state

Before committing to any platform on this list, verify two things directly with the vendor:

- Does the software support your state's CCDF submission format specifically?
- How quickly does the platform update when your state changes its subsidy rules?

Procare has the longest track record on both questions. For newer platforms, including PebbleDesk, you need direct confirmation. State subsidy requirements change, a platform that's current for Ohio may lag on a Texas rule change.

### What this list doesn't cover

This evaluation focuses on CCDF subsidy billing compliance. It does not rank platforms on parent communication features, enrollment management, or general billing for private-pay families. If your program is primarily private-pay with a small number of subsidized families, the ranking here may not match your priorities; Brightwheel's parent communication strengths matter more in that context.
