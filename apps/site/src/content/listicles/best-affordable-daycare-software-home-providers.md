## The Pricing Problem for Home Daycare Providers

Childcare management software is priced for centers. Per-child billing models, feature sets designed for multi-classroom operations, and admin tools built for directors with office staff. A home daycare provider serving 10 children does not need classroom management for four rooms or a parent broadcast system for 200 families.

What a home provider needs is straightforward: attendance tracking, basic billing, compliance records, and maybe parent communication. These features should not cost $100+ per month.

We built PebbleDesk's Home tier at {{plan.home.priceLabel}} because home providers are not small centers. They are a different operation with different needs and a different budget. The software should reflect that.

## How We Ranked These Options

Two factors: cost for a typical home daycare (6-15 children) and whether the compliance features match what state licensing actually requires. Platforms that cost center-level prices or lack core compliance tools ranked lower.

## The Options

### PebbleDesk

PebbleDesk's Home tier is designed specifically for providers with {{plan.home.capacityLabel}}. At {{plan.home.priceLabel}} rate, the cost stays the same whether you have 6 or 15 kids enrolled. Attendance tracking, ratio monitoring, and licensing records are included. The interface is built for a single-provider operation, not simplified down from a center version.

### Brightwheel

Brightwheel is the most recognized name in childcare software. Parents love it. The photo sharing, daily reports, and messaging are well-executed. For home providers, the cost is the issue. Per-child pricing for 10 children runs higher than flat-rate alternatives, and many features target center operations you do not run.

### Playground

Playground offers a modern interface for basic attendance and billing. Standard pricing is not published, and any no-cost state access should be verified directly. For home providers who do not bill subsidies and only need basic tools, Playground can be a reasonable starting point when the written quote or access program fits the budget. Compliance depth is limited, and subsidy reconciliation is not available.

### Sandbox (formerly Tadpoles)

Sandbox has been in the childcare software market for years. Digital check-in, daily reports, and basic compliance tools are available. The platform is designed for centers, and pricing is not transparent. Home providers may find the feature set more complex than needed.

### Paper and Spreadsheets

Many home providers still use paper sign-in sheets and spreadsheet billing. The monthly cost is zero. The risks are real: manual records lack automatic timestamps, they are harder to retrieve during inspections, and handwritten attendance logs are more prone to errors and disputes. For a provider who does not accept subsidies and operates in a state with minimal documentation requirements, paper can work. For everyone else, the compliance risk grows over time.
