## Why subsidy management is different

Most childcare software comparisons focus on parent communication, enrollment management, and general billing. Subsidy management is a separate category where mistakes have financial consequences.

CCDF, DHS vouchers, state pre-K subsidies, and Head Start grants all share the same requirement: demonstrate that you provided care, that the child was present, and that your billing matches your attendance records. When those pieces don't align, the subsidy agency takes the money back.

Programs that manage this well share one thing: attendance records and billing are in the same system, linked automatically.

### What subsidy-ready tools need that general tools don't

Brightwheel, Playground, and Kangarootime work for private-pay programs. Subsidy billing changes the requirements:

- Attendance records must be timestamped and tamper-evident
- Billing claims must be reconcilable against attendance
- Reports must satisfy state agency submission formats, not parent-readable formats
- The audit trail must show who recorded what, when, and whether anything changed

Most parent-communication-first tools were not built to provide this. Procare built subsidy workflows because directors managing state-funded programs demanded them. PebbleDesk is built with those requirements as the starting point.

### The cost of manual reconciliation

The real cost of manual subsidy management doesn't show up monthly. It shows up quarterly when a reimbursement is delayed because of a discrepancy, or annually when an audit finding requires a clawback.

A director spending 8 hours per month on manual reconciliation is spending roughly $80-200/month in labor (at $10-25/hour) to do work software handles automatically. Software often pays for itself in time savings before counting audit risk reduction.

### State-specific requirements

Subsidy programs vary by state. Your state's CCDF plan determines reimbursement rates, attendance tracking requirements, and submission formats. Before committing to any software for subsidy management, confirm:

1. Does it support your state's subsidy program submission format?
2. Does it generate the attendance documentation your state auditors expect?
3. How quickly does the platform update when your state changes its rules?

For Procare, these questions have been answered by years in the market. For newer platforms, they require direct confirmation with the vendor.
