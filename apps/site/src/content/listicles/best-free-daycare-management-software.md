### Quick recommendation

PebbleDesk is the best pick here for licensed providers who need attendance, guardian records, billing context, licensing documentation, and an audit trail more than a daycare app free forever. Spreadsheets are the only fully free option, while parent communication tools should be checked carefully for child-count limits, trial length, and public pricing.

### How we evaluated

We evaluated each option on free tier availability, whether access is a trial, parent communication, attendance, licensing records, and price transparency. We use published prices where vendors publish them. When pricing is not public, including when a vendor requires a quote, we say not published instead of estimating.

## What "free daycare app" usually means

When providers search for free apps for daycare providers, they are usually trying to solve several jobs at once: communicate with parents, track attendance, keep guardian information current, handle billing questions, and stay ready for licensing records requests.

The free-vs-trial reality is important. A tool may be free only for a short trial, free only under a very small child count, or free only if you accept manual spreadsheet work. That distinction matters more for licensed providers than for informal care because licensing visits and billing questions require organized records.

## Parent communication is only one part of the workflow

A daycare app to communicate with parents can reduce phone calls and message threads, especially when families need daily reports, pickup reminders, or billing updates. But parent communication does not replace the director-side record system.

For licensed centers and in-home daycare providers, the same child file often needs guardian contacts, authorized pickup details, attendance history, billing context, and documentation that can be reviewed later. PebbleDesk is framed here as a director and staff workspace for those operational records, not as a separate consumer-grade parent app or photo feed.

## Attendance and licensing decide whether free is enough

Free spreadsheets can record attendance, but they do not automatically create a reliable audit trail. Staff still have to maintain timestamps, updates, guardian changes, and billing notes consistently. If one person forgets the process, the record becomes harder to defend during a licensing review.

That is why a free daycare app can be enough for a very small private-pay program but not enough for many licensed providers. Once a program needs attendance history, licensing evidence, billing context, and parent communication in the same workflow, a low-cost paid system is usually more realistic than a free-only setup.

## Recommendation by provider type

**Very small private-pay program:** Start with spreadsheets or a limited free option if licensing requirements are light and parent communication is simple.

**Licensed in-home daycare:** Choose a tool that keeps attendance, guardian records, billing context, and licensing documentation together. PebbleDesk Home is the best pick here for that operational use case after the trial.

**Center comparing parent communication tools:** Review the parent communication list and confirm pricing in writing when it is not published.
