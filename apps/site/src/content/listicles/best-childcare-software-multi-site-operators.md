## Why Multi-Site Operators Need Different Software

A single childcare center can get by with almost any management software. The director knows the operation personally. Data gaps are covered by daily observation. Reports are assembled from one location's records.

Multi-site operators do not have that luxury. The central office needs visibility across 3, 10, or 50 locations without visiting each one. Compliance data, financial performance, enrollment trends, and staffing gaps need to be visible from a dashboard, not assembled from phone calls and emailed spreadsheets.

We built PebbleDesk's multi-site support for this exact situation. When every site runs the same system, cross-location reporting stops being a monthly project and becomes a real-time capability.

## How We Ranked These Platforms

Three criteria: cross-site compliance reporting, centralized subsidy billing, and total cost at scale. Platforms that treat multi-site as an afterthought or that scale expensively on per-child pricing ranked lower.

## The Platforms

### PebbleDesk

PebbleDesk supports sales-led multi-site rollouts with centralized dashboards showing ratio compliance, attendance, and staff credentials across all locations. Subsidy reconciliation runs at the organization level, matching billing claims against attendance data from every site. When a board member or funder asks for aggregated data, it is a report pull, not a multi-day data assembly project.

### Procare

Procare has served multi-site childcare operators for years. The feature set is deep, and the multi-site administration tools are functional. The challenge is the interface. Staff training takes longer than modern platforms, and the cloud migration is still underway. For operators who value stability and track record over interface design, Procare remains a reliable choice.

### Brightwheel

Brightwheel offers a multi-center view for organizations, but its architecture is parent-engagement-first. Cross-site compliance reporting is limited compared to purpose-built administrative platforms. Per-child pricing also scales expensively for operators with hundreds of enrolled children across multiple sites.

### Kangarootime

Kangarootime handles multi-site billing and payment processing well. For operators where billing is the primary software need, it delivers. Compliance features like cross-site ratio tracking and aggregated audit reports are secondary, which limits its value for operators in heavily regulated states.

### ChildCareCRM

ChildCareCRM solves a different problem: enrollment management. It tracks leads, manages tours, and handles waitlists across locations. It does not manage daily operations, attendance, or compliance. Multi-site operators often pair ChildCareCRM with an operational platform, effectively running two systems.
