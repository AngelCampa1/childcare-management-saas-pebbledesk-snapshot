## Why Subsidy Billing Is Where Most Software Falls Short

Private-pay tuition is straightforward. Parent owes $1,200/month. Invoice generated. Payment collected. Done.

Subsidy billing is different. The state pays part of the tuition. The parent pays a co-pay. The amounts change when a family's eligibility is redetermined. The billing cycle doesn't always match the month. And the state agency has a specific format it requires for billing submissions.

Every piece of childcare software handles private-pay tuition competently. Very few handle the subsidy billing workflow without requiring manual workarounds.

## The Manual Reconciliation Problem

Directors running centers with 20-50% subsidized enrollment often describe the same situation: they use their childcare software for everything; enrollment, parent communication, attendance, but they maintain a separate spreadsheet for subsidy billing reconciliation because the software doesn't produce the format the state agency needs.

That's a significant administrative burden. It means double-entry, which means errors. It means time that could be spent on the floor spent in spreadsheets.

The goal of childcare software is to eliminate that parallel process, not just reduce it.

## What Good Subsidy Billing Support Actually Looks Like

When evaluating whether a platform genuinely handles subsidy billing; rather than having a checkbox feature that still requires manual work; look for these specifics:

Can you generate a billing submission in your state agency's required format directly from the system?

Does the platform handle mixed-funding families in a single enrollment record; private-pay portion, subsidy portion, and co-pay tracked together?

When a family's subsidy eligibility changes, does the billing update automatically or do you do it manually?

Can you reconcile what the state paid against what you billed without leaving the platform?

Most products answer "no" to most of these. The ones that answer "yes" are worth the evaluation time.
