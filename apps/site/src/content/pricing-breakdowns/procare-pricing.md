## What Procare actually costs

Procare's pricing is modular: the number you see advertised depends on which pieces you need. Directors should confirm which plan covers their program type, whether parent communication is included, and which implementation or training services are required.

Realistic all-in cost for a licensed center requires current written terms before implementation or training.

### Add-on structure

Parent communication is not bundled with the base management plan. Implementation support and staff training modules are priced separately. The quote you get during a demo will not reflect what year one costs. Ask explicitly about implementation and onboarding fees before signing.

### Where Procare is strong

Procare has been in the market long enough to build real subsidy billing infrastructure. CCDF billing workflows, subsidy agency reporting, and attendance records for licensing audits have more depth here than in newer entrants. Centers with staff already trained on Procare have real switching costs, and institutional knowledge of the system's quirks has value.

### Where Procare shows its age

The interface was built for desktop software and has been adapted for cloud and mobile over time. New staff typically need more training to get operational than with cloud-native alternatives. State subsidy rule changes can take time to surface in Procare's workflows. Directors in states with frequent CCDF updates sometimes end up bridging the gap manually.

### The acquisition context

In February 2024, Roper Technologies acquired Procare for $1.86 billion; approximately 18 times EBITDA. Roper is a conglomerate with 50+ industrial software companies. The model is to acquire profitable platforms and optimize for margin.

What this means for pricing: institutional ownership with a margin mandate is a predictor of price increases, not decreases. Directors locking in multi-year contracts with Procare today should factor in the trajectory, not just the current rate.

What this means for support: directors should ask which support channels are included, whether phone support is available on their plan, and what escalation path exists for time-sensitive billing issues. Post-acquisition margin optimization rarely means more support staff.

This is not a reason to avoid Procare if it is already working for your center. It is information to weigh when comparing total cost of ownership over three to five years.
