## What Jackrabbit Care is

Jackrabbit Care is class and session management software. It started in the gymnastics studio market and expanded to swim schools, dance studios, and enrichment programs. Its feature set reflects those origins: enrollment management, session billing, family accounts, attendance tracking, and QuickBooks integration.

It is not designed for state-licensed childcare centers. CCDF billing, staff-to-child ratio tracking, and state licensing audit documentation are not part of Jackrabbit Care's feature set. If your program holds a state childcare license and bills subsidy agencies, Jackrabbit Care doesn't cover those requirements.

## How the pricing works

Jackrabbit Care uses a per-location flat rate.

**1-5 locations:** $69 per location per month. A single-location program pays $69/month regardless of how many children are enrolled. This makes the pricing highly favorable for larger programs at each site.

**6-20 locations:** $49 per location per month. At 6 locations, the monthly cost drops from $345 to $294. Operators planning expansion to a 6th location should consider timing the switch to coincide with launch.

QuickBooks integration is included at both tiers. Processing fees apply on top of the subscription; confirm current card processing rates directly with Jackrabbit Care.

## Per-location vs. per-child: which is better?

The per-location model favors large programs at each site. A single location with 80 children pays the same $69/month as one with 20 children.

Per-child pricing at $2/student (Playground) costs $40/month for 20 children and $160/month for 80 children. At 80 enrolled children, Jackrabbit Care at $69/month is less than half the cost of Playground's per-student model.

The comparison changes for multi-location operators. A 10-location operator pays $490/month with Jackrabbit Care (at $49/location). With Playground at $2/student and an average of 50 children per location, the same operator pays $1,000/month.

## What licensed childcare directors need to know

If you run a licensed childcare center:

1. Jackrabbit Care doesn't support CCDF billing. You'll need a separate process for subsidy reconciliation.
2. Ratio tracking is not a Jackrabbit Care feature. State-required staff-to-child ratios must be tracked manually.
3. Audit documentation for state licensing inspections is not formatted for state officer requirements.

These gaps don't make Jackrabbit Care a bad product; it's a good product for its target market. They make it the wrong product for licensed childcare compliance.
