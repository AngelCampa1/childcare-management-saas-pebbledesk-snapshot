## How Kangarootime pricing works

Kangarootime uses a per-classroom pricing model: $8 per classroom per month. That is the published rate; unusual in an industry where most competitors price by enrolled child count or charge a flat rate.

Cost by classroom count:

| Classrooms | Monthly Cost | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| 3 rooms | $24/mo | Typical small center |
| 5 rooms | $40/mo | Common growing-center layout |
| 6 rooms | $48/mo | Common mid-size layout |
| 8 rooms | $64/mo | Larger center footprint |
| 10 rooms | $80/mo | Multi-room center footprint |
| 12+ rooms | $96+/mo | Larger center |

Payment processing is separate from the subscription. Tuition collected through Kangarootime's billing system incurs transaction fees on top of these figures.

### Why the per-classroom model matters for growing centers

At three classrooms, Kangarootime is the most affordable option in the market at $24/month. That changes as centers grow.

A center that expands from 5 rooms to 8 rooms adds $24/month to their software bill immediately, before the new classrooms reach enrollment capacity. For an operator planning a buildout or adding infant or toddler capacity, that cost impact is predictable but often unbudgeted.

The per-classroom model also creates a misalignment between the cost driver (rooms, a physical infrastructure decision) and the revenue driver (enrolled children). A center can add children to existing rooms without cost increase, but opening one new room costs $8/month regardless of how many children are in it.

### Comparing to flat-rate alternatives

At 8-10 classrooms, Kangarootime's cost ($64-80/month) approaches PebbleDesk Center Starter's {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}} rate. The difference is that PebbleDesk includes ratio compliance and audit documentation, and does not scale the subscription cost when you add a classroom.

For a center currently at 5-6 classrooms and growing, the cost trajectory of a per-classroom model versus a flat rate is worth running before signing a Kangarootime annual contract.

### What Kangarootime includes

Kangarootime covers child check-in/check-out organized by room, parent communication, daily activity logs, tuition billing, and enrollment management. The parent-facing features are the product's primary selling point. Compliance documentation, ratio tracking, audit-ready exports, and subsidy reconciliation are not the platform's core focus.

Centers choosing Kangarootime primarily for its price at small classroom counts should factor in whether compliance needs will require supplementary tools at additional cost.
