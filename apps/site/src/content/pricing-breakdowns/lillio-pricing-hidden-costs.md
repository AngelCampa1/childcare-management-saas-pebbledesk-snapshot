## What Lillio actually costs

Lillio; formerly HiMama before the 2023 rebrand; does not publish its pricing. The number you see requires a demo call and a written quote.

Before signing, ask Lillio for a written quote that separates subscription, implementation, billing cadence, processing fees, and any parent-app or admin-user limits.

| Program Size | Pricing Note |
|-------------|-------------------------------|
| Small center (under 30 children) | Written quote required |
| Mid-size center (30-60 children) | Written quote required |
| Larger program (60+ children) | Written quote required |

Ask whether your quote is for annual or monthly billing. Annual contracts are typically 15-20% cheaper, which means the quoted monthly rate may reflect a commitment of 12 months, not a true month-to-month price.

### What Lillio is built for

Lillio's core product is parent engagement: daily reports, photo and video sharing, observation notes, and parent messaging. If the primary problem you're trying to solve is keeping parents informed about their child's day, Lillio is a reasonable candidate.

The platform's limitations show up in compliance and operations. Ratio tracking, licensing audit exports, and subsidy reconciliation are not Lillio's focus. Centers that need those workflows often layer a second tool on top, a binder system for ratios, a spreadsheet for subsidy, a separate billing platform.

### The two-tool problem

The hidden cost that does not appear in any Lillio quote is the second tool. Directors who use Lillio for parent communication and then separately manage compliance documents, ratio logs, and subsidy billing through other systems are paying in both money and time.

One licensed center director managing 45 children described it this way: Lillio handles parent communication and Procare handles billing and compliance, but that is two monthly subscriptions, two logins, and two data imports every time a child's information changes.

### Payment processing

Lillio charges transaction fees when tuition is collected through their billing system. These fees are separate from the subscription. For centers collecting $10,000-20,000/month in tuition, processing fees add meaningfully to the effective monthly cost.

### How PebbleDesk compares

PebbleDesk Center Starter is {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}} for licensed single-site centers. Billing, ratio compliance, attendance, and audit documentation are part of the same product. There is no second tool for compliance and no separate billing platform for tuition collection beyond standard Stripe processing fees.

For centers currently evaluating Lillio, the subscription cost comparison is close. The question is whether you want a parent communication tool that requires supplementary compliance systems, or a single platform built around compliance first with parent communication included.
