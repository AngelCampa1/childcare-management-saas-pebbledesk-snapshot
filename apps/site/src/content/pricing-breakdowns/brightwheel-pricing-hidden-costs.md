## Start With the Written Quote

Brightwheel requires a written quote for current subscription pricing. That means a licensed childcare center cannot budget from a public monthly number alone.

A licensed center with multiple classrooms, online tuition collection, subsidy billing questions, and compliance reporting needs should ask for all pricing terms in writing before comparing vendors.

Here is how the cost can compound for a licensed center director.

## The Enrollment Scaling Problem

Brightwheel's quote should make clear whether pricing changes with enrollment count, modules, payment volume, contract length, or support level.

The implication: every time you fill a waitlist spot and successfully grow enrollment, you should know whether your subscription, payment fees, or support requirements change. Do not assume a quote for today's census will still match next fall's operating model.

## Payment Processing on Top

Brightwheel payment processing is separate from the subscription. Your exact fee depends on program-specific payment terms, payment method, and billing settings. A card-processing benchmark of 2.9% plus $0.30 per transaction would add about $444/month for a center collecting $15,000/month across 30 card transactions.

Directors who compare Brightwheel's subscription cost against competitors often don't account for payment processing, which can double the effective monthly cost for centers with high online payment adoption.

## What the Tiers Don't Tell You

The compliance features that matter most to a licensed center director, including proactive ratio alerts, subsidy billing reconciliation, and audit-ready documentation, should be demonstrated in the exact plan named in the quote.

Brightwheel was built as a parent communication platform that added operations features over time. The product's architecture reflects that origin. Compliance tooling was added later and is not the core product experience.

For a director evaluating software specifically to manage licensing compliance, the demo should prove the workflow before the price conversation ends.

## What the Review Record Shows

Brightwheel reviews vary by platform and change over time. Directors should read current review pages while evaluating the written quote, paying special attention to billing timing, cancellation terms, and support responsiveness.

## The Full-Cost Comparison

Before signing a Brightwheel contract, calculate the true monthly cost:

1. Subscription tier based on your enrollment count
2. Payment processing fees on tuition collected through the platform
3. Any supplemental tools you'll need for subsidy billing and compliance documentation that Brightwheel doesn't cover
