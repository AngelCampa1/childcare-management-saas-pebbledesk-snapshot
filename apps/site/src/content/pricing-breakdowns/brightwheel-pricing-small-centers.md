## What Brightwheel costs for small centers

Brightwheel pricing is not listed publicly. You have to contact their sales team to get a number. Ask for the quote in writing and confirm which features, billing cadence, cancellation terms, and payment processing fees are included.

For programs under 30 children, compare written quotes across enrollment levels:

| Enrollment | Subscription Question |
|------------|-------------------------------|
| ~10 children | What is the current subscription quote? |
| ~20 children | Does the plan change at 20 children? |
| ~30 children | Does the plan change at 30 children? |

Your actual quote may differ based on region, features selected, and the sales conversation.

### Payment processing is a separate cost

Every center under 30 children should confirm Brightwheel payment service fees in writing. Those fees run on top of the subscription regardless of enrollment size.

A 20-child center collecting $8,000/month in tuition would model roughly $232/month under standard online card benchmark math, separate from the subscription quote. The actual Brightwheel payment service fees should be confirmed in writing.

### What Brightwheel is built around

Brightwheel was designed for parent engagement. The photo feed, daily activity reports, and parent messaging are the product's core strengths. Small centers where parent communication is the primary problem often find those features worth the cost.

The compliance side is a different story. Ratio tracking, licensing audit exports, and subsidy reconciliation are not Brightwheel's focus. Directors in licensed centers managing state inspections typically build manual workarounds outside the platform for that work.

### How PebbleDesk compares at this size

PebbleDesk Center Starter is {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}} for licensed single-site centers. There are no per-child fees. Payment processing runs through standard Stripe fees, not added to the subscription.

For a 30-child center using Brightwheel, the subscription quote and the payment service fees on tuition should be modeled separately. As a benchmark, standard online card math on $10,000/month in tuition is about $290/month before the subscription is added.

If state licensing compliance; ratios, attendance records, audit documentation; is your primary operational pressure, that should drive the tool selection more than the parent communication feature set.
