## The Pricing Transparency Problem

ChildPilot does not publish pricing. To learn what the platform costs, you need to contact their sales team and go through a pricing conversation. For directors comparing multiple platforms, this adds friction to the evaluation process.

PebbleDesk publishes pricing: {{plan.home.priceLabel}} for Home and {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}} for Center Starter. What you see is what you pay.

## How to evaluate a ChildPilot quote

Ask for the monthly software fee, required setup services, payment processing terms, and whether billing, attendance, parent messaging, and reporting are all included in the same plan. The important comparison is not only the first quoted subscription price. Directors also need to know whether ratio documentation, subsidy follow-up, and exports are available without moving into a higher tier.

For a small center, the quote should be easy to defend against enrollment size. A platform can look affordable when it covers attendance and invoices, then become harder to justify if compliance work still has to happen in spreadsheets. PebbleDesk's pricing page is meant to remove that ambiguity before the demo.
