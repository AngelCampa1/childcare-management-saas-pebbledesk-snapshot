## The Pricing Transparency Problem

Famly does not publish pricing. To learn what the platform costs, you need to contact their sales team and go through a pricing conversation. For directors comparing multiple platforms, this adds friction to the evaluation process.

PebbleDesk publishes pricing: {{plan.home.priceLabel}} for Home and {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}} for Center Starter. What you see is what you pay.

## What US centers should ask before comparing costs

Famly's product story is strong around family engagement and nursery management, but US childcare operators should separate parent communication value from US compliance cost. Ask whether the quoted plan includes reports that support state licensing reviews, subsidy attendance exports, billing reconciliation, and the workflows your team uses during audits.

The budget question is also operational. A sales-led quote may be reasonable for a larger program, but directors still need a clear all-in number before they can compare Famly with platforms that publish pricing. PebbleDesk keeps the entry point visible so a center can decide whether the savings in compliance cleanup justify the subscription.
