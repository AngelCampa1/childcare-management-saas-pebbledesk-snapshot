## Procare pricing for multi-location operators

Procare does not publish its pricing. Quotes require a sales engagement, and multi-location pricing involves more variables than a single-site subscription: per-location licensing, implementation scope, training packages, and data migration.

Ask Procare for these line items in writing:

| Sites | Subscription Quote | Implementation Quote |
|-------|-------------------------------|--------------------------|
| 1 location | Required | Required |
| 2-3 locations | Required | Required |
| 5+ locations | Required | Required |

Actual quotes will vary based on features selected, negotiation, and contract length.

### Per-location licensing is the core structure

Procare prices each site as a separate unit. For a growing organization opening a second or third location, that means returning to the sales process each time, not self-serve activation. Operators describe this as a friction point when growth timelines are compressed.

The practical effect: budgeting for Procare at scale requires forecasting both the recurring subscription cost per location and the one-time setup cost each time a new site opens.

### Implementation costs are real and separate

Multi-location implementations involve data migration from prior systems, staff training, and configuration work that Procare treats as billable services. Request implementation costs separately from the subscription before the first month of regular billing starts.

Training packages add to that. Procare offers tiered training options at additional cost; relevant for operators onboarding staff at each new location.

### Annual maintenance and contract structure

Some Procare contracts include annual maintenance fees on top of the monthly subscription. Review the contract structure carefully before signing, particularly how auto-renewal is handled and what the notice window is for cancellation.

### PebbleDesk for multi-site

PebbleDesk multi-site rollouts are custom-priced for operators. There are no per-child fees and no per-location setup fees beyond the contract terms. Payment processing runs through standard Stripe rates.

If you are evaluating Procare for a multi-location operation, requesting a PebbleDesk multi-site quote provides a direct comparison before committing to an implementation engagement.
