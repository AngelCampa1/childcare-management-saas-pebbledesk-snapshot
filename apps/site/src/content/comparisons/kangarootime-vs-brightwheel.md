## Two Pricing Models, Same Compliance Gaps

Kangarootime and Brightwheel represent different pricing approaches to childcare management. Kangarootime's per-class model should be confirmed in the current quote. Brightwheel uses written-quote subscription pricing, so directors should compare the actual subscription, payment, renewal, and support terms before budgeting.

Both platforms cover the operational basics: attendance, check-in/check-out, billing, and parent communication. Brightwheel does parent communication better. Kangarootime does staff scheduling better.

Neither solves the compliance problems that actually create risk for directors: ratio violations during transitions and subsidy billing delays from manual reconciliation.

## The Compliance Gap Both Share

A licensing auditor does not care which platform has the better parent app. They care about ratio documentation and attendance records. Both Kangarootime and Brightwheel track attendance. Neither proactively alerts staff when a room approaches its ratio limit.
