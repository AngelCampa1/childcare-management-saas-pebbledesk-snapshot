## Different Programs, Different Tools

Tadpoles is a photo-sharing app first. The product is built around the moments parents want from their child's day, a picture at lunch, a note about a milestone, a quick update before pickup. For an informal program where compliance is light or absent, that is the entire job.

PebbleDesk is a compliance platform first. The product is built around the parts of running a licensed center that carry citation risk and reimbursement risk. Real-time ratio alerts. State-formatted CCDF and DHS voucher reports. One-click audit documentation that hands a licensing inspector exactly what they ask for.

These are not competing products in the usual sense. They are answers to different questions about what childcare software should be.

## When Each Wins

Tadpoles wins when the program is unlicensed or informal, a small in-home program below the state licensing threshold, a church-based mothers-day-out program without state oversight, or an enrichment program where compliance is not the binding constraint. The setup is fast, the parent experience is good, and the price fits a program that is not generating significant revenue.

PebbleDesk wins when the program is a US licensed childcare center. State licensing inspections happen on a recurring schedule. CCDF or DHS voucher families create monthly subsidy billing work. Ratio compliance is a citation risk every day. Tadpoles does not address any of those costs. PebbleDesk addresses each of them as a core product feature.

## The Honest Recommendation

If you are running an informal program and Tadpoles fits, stay on Tadpoles. The cost is low, the setup is fast, and the photo experience is good. Switching to a compliance platform you do not need is wasted money.

If you are running a licensed center and currently on Tadpoles, the math is the other way. The hidden cost; three to four hours per month on subsidy reformatting, weekend audit prep, and the citation risk from manual ratio tracking; is more than {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}}. PebbleDesk replaces that work directly.
