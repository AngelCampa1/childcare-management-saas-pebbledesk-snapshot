## The Activity Platform Adapted for Childcare

Jackrabbit originally built management software for dance studios. The enrollment workflow, a parent registers a child for a class, sets up a payment method, and the system handles billing and roster management; translated well enough to childcare that Jackrabbit Care emerged as an adaptation for that market.

The class management features are genuinely good. Enrollment, scheduling, family billing, and account management are mature because they were built for activity businesses that live and die by efficient class enrollment. For a childcare program structured around defined class sessions; enrichment programs, after-school programs, or summer camps; Jackrabbit Care works well.

The problem is that licensed childcare compliance is not a class management problem. A state licensing officer does not ask for your enrollment roster. They ask for ratio records, staff credentials, and attendance documentation formatted for state review.

## The Parent Communication Platform Without Operations Depth

Lillio (formerly HiMama) was purpose-built for what early childhood educators actually do: observe children, document learning, communicate with families, and track developmental progress. The platform reflects a genuine understanding of early childhood practice.

For directors who want educators to feel supported in their professional documentation work, Lillio's curriculum and observation tools are better than anything Jackrabbit Care provides. The parent engagement angle is also genuinely strong; families who use Lillio's app stay connected to their child's learning in a meaningful way.

What Lillio was not built for is the operational compliance layer that licensed childcare centers must maintain for state licensing audits. Ratio monitoring, staff certification tracking, and subsidy billing reconciliation are outside Lillio's design scope.

## Where the Gaps Meet

A licensed childcare center using Jackrabbit Care for enrollment and billing and Lillio for parent communication has paid for coverage of the parent-facing and billing layers. The licensing compliance layer is still unaddressed:

- No real-time ratio alerts in either system
- No staff credential tracking for state licensing requirements
- No subsidy billing reconciliation formatted for state CCDF submission
- No audit documentation export formatted for a state inspector

That compliance layer is exactly what PebbleDesk is built to cover.
