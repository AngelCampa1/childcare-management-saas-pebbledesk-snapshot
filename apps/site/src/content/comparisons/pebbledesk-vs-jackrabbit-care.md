## Built For Different Programs

Jackrabbit was a scheduling product for gymnastics gyms before it was anything else. The class-rotation engine, the session-based billing, and the family account model all carry that DNA. When the company extended into childcare with Jackrabbit Care, the scheduling foundation came with it.

That foundation is genuinely strong for programs that run on rotating sessions. It is less aligned with a licensed childcare center where the same children attend the same classroom every weekday and the binding constraint is staff-to-child ratio rather than session enrollment.

PebbleDesk was built for licensed childcare from day one. The product surface assumes age-banded classrooms, daily attendance, ratio compliance, and state subsidy reimbursement. Every feature exists because a director identified it as a recurring source of risk or wasted time.

## What Compliance Looks Like in Each

Jackrabbit Care has reporting. Reports are pulled, reviewed, and shared. That model fits a gymnastics gym where compliance is loosely defined and the worst case is a scheduling conflict.

A licensed childcare center has a different worst case. A classroom out of ratio for an hour is a citation. A CCDF reimbursement delayed by a reformatting error is a six-week cash flow gap. An audit response missing one attendance record is a follow-up inspection. PebbleDesk surfaces those situations in real time; alerts when ratios slip, state-formatted exports for subsidy billing, one-click audit documentation that matches what inspectors actually request.
