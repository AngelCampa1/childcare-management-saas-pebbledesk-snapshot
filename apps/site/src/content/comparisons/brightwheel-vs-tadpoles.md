## Pricing comparison

Brightwheel does not publish one current subscription table for every program. You request a quote to get a number, then confirm processing fees, onboarding, renewal terms, and whether compliance workflows are included in the quoted plan.

Tadpoles has a free plan for basic daily reports and parent communication. Premium features, enhanced observations, unlimited photo storage, advanced reporting, portfolio exports, are on a paid plan that also does not list pricing publicly.

Neither product makes it easy to comparison shop without a sales call.

## What each platform does well

Brightwheel's parent communication is its defining feature. Photos, daily activity reports, messaging, and billing live in one parent-facing app. The enrollment management and invoicing tools are functional. For a private-pay center where parent engagement and tuition collection are the primary software needs, Brightwheel handles both.

Tadpoles does daily reports well and does them for free. A home daycare provider or small preschool that runs on family relationships and referrals gets a polished parent experience at no cost. The platform is simple to set up and simple for families to use.

## Where both fall short for licensed programs

The gap is the same for both platforms: they were built to manage the center's relationship with parents, not with state licensing agencies.

**Ratio tracking.** State licensing requires maintaining staff-to-child ratios throughout the operating day, not just at check-in and check-out. Neither Brightwheel nor Tadpoles monitors or documents ratios continuously. When a licensing officer arrives and asks for ratio records at a specific time, both platforms leave directors without a direct answer.

**Subsidy reconciliation.** Centers serving CCDF families need monthly billing documentation that matches attendance records and voucher authorizations. Brightwheel has some subsidy features but directors report having to manually export and reformat data before submission. Tadpoles has no subsidy workflow at any price point.

**Audit-ready documentation.** Both platforms generate records for parents. Licensing inspectors need records in a different format: time-stamped attendance, room-by-room ratio logs, and subsidy billing records that match reimbursement claims. Directors on both platforms typically maintain a parallel paper system to cover the audit side.

**Offline access.** Neither platform works without internet. A licensing inspection during a connectivity interruption leaves directors without access to the records the inspector is asking for.

## Verdict

Brightwheel fits centers that prioritize parent communication and private-pay tuition collection, have enough enrollment that a per-child subscription makes sense, and do not have significant subsidy billing obligations.

Tadpoles free fits small programs, home daycares or small preschools, where parent engagement is the primary software need and the budget for monthly software is limited.

For licensed centers with CCDF families, regular licensing audits, or both, PebbleDesk publishes flat-rate plans and shows {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}} on the pricing page, and is built around the compliance workflow first: continuous ratio tracking, audit-ready attendance records, and subsidy reconciliation without the manual export step.
