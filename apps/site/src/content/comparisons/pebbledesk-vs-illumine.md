## Two Different Definitions of "Childcare Software"

Illumine grew up serving preschools and daycares in India and has expanded internationally. The product reflects that origin in the best way; learning portfolios, observation tracking, and parent communication are first-class features because that is what the home market valued.

PebbleDesk grew up serving US licensed childcare centers. The product reflects that origin in a different way. State-formatted CCDF reports, real-time ratio alerts mapped to state age-band rules, and one-click audit documentation are first-class features because those are the recurring costs that US-licensed-center directors face.

Both platforms can serve the other market in some form. Neither is the best choice when used outside its native domain.

## When Illumine Is The Right Answer

If your program is an unlicensed preschool, an enrichment-focused private program, or an international school, Illumine is a strong choice. The learning portfolios are genuinely beautiful. Parent adoption is high because the app is polished. The compliance gaps do not matter because the program is not state-licensed and does not take CCDF vouchers.

## When PebbleDesk Is The Right Answer

If your program is a US licensed childcare center, the math goes the other way. Ratio compliance is a citation risk. Subsidy reimbursement timing is a cash flow risk. Audit preparation is a recurring time cost. PebbleDesk's compliance features address those costs directly, and the {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}} flat pricing is less than half the upper end of Illumine's range. The trade-off is a less polished learning portfolio, which, for most US licensed centers, is not the deciding factor.
