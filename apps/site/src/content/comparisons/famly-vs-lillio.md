## International Platforms, US Compliance Needs

Famly and Lillio both built strong childcare platforms in their home markets (Europe and Canada respectively). Both are expanding into the US. Both have polished parent engagement features.

The challenge for US center directors is that compliance requirements are deeply state-specific. California CCLD audits, Texas DFPS inspections, and New York OCFS requirements all have different documentation formats, ratio standards, and subsidy billing processes.

Platforms designed for European or Canadian regulatory frameworks may cover the broad strokes of attendance and documentation but miss the specific requirements that US licensing inspectors check.
