## Famly vs PebbleDesk: Beautiful UI vs US Compliance Reality

Famly is one of the best-designed products in childcare software. The parent app is consistently praised, the interface feels modern, and the learning documentation is well-built. For UK and European centers, Famly is a strong default choice.

For US centers, the story is more complicated. Famly was not designed around US-specific compliance, including CCDF subsidy vouchers, state DHS reporting formats, and state licensing audit requirements that vary by state. Those workflows are exactly what create the most director time and the most regulatory risk in US-licensed childcare.

PebbleDesk is built for that US-specific reality. State-formatted subsidy reports, real-time ratio alerts tied to your state's ratio rules, audit documentation that matches what state inspectors actually request. The trade is a simpler family communication workflow.
