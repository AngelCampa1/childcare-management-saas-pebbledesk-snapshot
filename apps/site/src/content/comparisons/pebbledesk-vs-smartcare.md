## PebbleDesk vs SmartCare: Compliance vs Billing Depth

SmartCare built a billing-focused platform. Tuition variations, late fee logic, multi-child discount rules, payment plans, autopay, and processing are well-developed. Centers with complex invoicing requirements often pick SmartCare for that depth.

PebbleDesk built a compliance-focused platform. Real-time ratio alerts when a classroom is at risk. CCDF and DHS subsidy reports formatted for state submission. One-click audit documentation. A flat published monthly price that does not scale within plan caps: Home at {{plan.home.priceLabel}}, Center Starter at {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}}, and Center Pro at {{plan.center_pro.priceLabel}}.

The two products do not have identical strengths. The right choice depends on which problem is bigger.

## When SmartCare Makes Sense

If you have unusual billing, such as tuition rules that other platforms cannot represent cleanly, payment plans with custom logic, or complex multi-child discounts, SmartCare's billing depth is real and may be worth a higher quote. Centers where billing administration takes hours per week often find that depth pays back.

## When PebbleDesk Makes Sense

If you take CCDF or DHS vouchers, get inspected by state licensing, or have had ratio issues, PebbleDesk's compliance focus solves problems that SmartCare addresses partially or with manual workarounds. Compare PebbleDesk's published price against SmartCare's current written quote, especially if billing is standard but compliance is a constant concern.

## The Pricing Trade-off

A 50-child center can evaluate PebbleDesk's published Center Starter and Center Pro prices before a sales call. SmartCare requires a current written quote before directors can calculate the annual difference. Whether SmartCare's billing depth justifies a higher contract depends on whether your billing actually needs it.
