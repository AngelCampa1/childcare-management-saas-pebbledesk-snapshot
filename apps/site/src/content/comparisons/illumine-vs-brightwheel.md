## The Same Bet, Different Price Tags

Illumine and Brightwheel made the same fundamental design bet: build the parent experience first and build operational features around it. Both have daily reports, photo sharing, parent messaging, digital enrollment, and billing. Both lack real-time ratio alerts and US-formatted compliance documentation.

The difference is price and origin. Brightwheel is US-native and priced by written quote. Illumine positions itself as a cheaper option built on similar capabilities.

## When the Illumine Pricing Argument Works

For a center that has been using Brightwheel for two or three years and has watched the monthly cost grow with enrollment, Illumine's pricing pitch lands. If the features you actually use are daily reports, parent messaging, and tuition invoicing, and Illumine delivers those at lower cost, the case for switching is financially straightforward.

The parent communication features in both platforms are comparable at the surface level. Parents receive daily reports either way. Photo sharing and in-app messaging work in both. A family adopting a new platform will adjust quickly.

## Where Brightwheel's US Presence Matters

Brightwheel has broader US adoption, which creates a practical advantage: some parents have already used Brightwheel at a previous center or preschool. That familiarity reduces onboarding friction.

Brightwheel's US-based support team responds during US business hours. For a director with an urgent billing question the day before a subsidy deadline, that availability matters more than any feature comparison.

Brightwheel also has more integrations and community resources in the US market. Third-party tools, director communities, and implementation guides are better developed for Brightwheel than for Illumine in the US context.

## The Compliance Gap Both Share

The conversation changes when the question is state licensing compliance. A state inspector arriving at either a Brightwheel or Illumine center will ask for documentation that neither platform produces automatically:

- Real-time ratio records for the inspection period
- Staff certification records under state licensing standards
- Subsidy billing records formatted for state agency review

Both platforms store the underlying data. Neither turns it into the formatted documentation a licensing officer requests without manual work. For directors who have been through a licensing visit, that gap is the deciding factor.
