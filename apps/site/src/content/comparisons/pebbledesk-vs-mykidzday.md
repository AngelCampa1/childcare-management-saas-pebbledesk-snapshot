## PebbleDesk vs MyKidzDay: Same Segment, Different Feature Depth

MyKidzDay and PebbleDesk both target small to mid-size licensed childcare centers. The pricing tiers overlap, the customer profile overlaps, and on the surface the products solve similar problems.

The depth differs significantly. MyKidzDay covers attendance tracking and parent daily reports. PebbleDesk covers director-side attendance, family records, and the compliance work that takes the most director time: ratio monitoring, subsidy billing, and audit preparation.

## What MyKidzDay Misses

Real-time ratio alerts. When a teacher leaves a classroom and coverage drops below the licensed ratio, MyKidzDay does not flag it. The director either monitors manually or finds out later; sometimes from a licensing inspector.

State-formatted CCDF and DHS subsidy reports. MyKidzDay does not produce billing files pre-formatted for state agency submission. Centers using it maintain spreadsheets to reconcile subsidy attendance every billing cycle.

One-click audit documentation. When a licensing inspector arrives, MyKidzDay users assemble reports manually. PebbleDesk generates the attendance, ratio, and incident records inspectors ask for in one export.

## When MyKidzDay Still Makes Sense

If you have used MyKidzDay for years, your workflows are stable, your operation is small and simple, and you do not deal with subsidy or compliance pain; staying put is reasonable. Stable software your team knows is genuinely valuable. For directors evaluating fresh, the feature and price comparison usually points the other direction.
