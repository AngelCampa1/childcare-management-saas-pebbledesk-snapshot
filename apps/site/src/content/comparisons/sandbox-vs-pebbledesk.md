## Different Problems, Different Platforms

Sandbox and PebbleDesk represent two different answers to the question: what should childcare software prioritize?

Sandbox says: the parent experience. A modern app that parents enjoy using drives engagement and differentiates the center.

PebbleDesk says: the director compliance burden. Ratio monitoring, subsidy tracking, and audit documentation protect the license and the cash flow.

Both answers are valid. The right choice depends on which problem is costing you more time and creating more risk.
