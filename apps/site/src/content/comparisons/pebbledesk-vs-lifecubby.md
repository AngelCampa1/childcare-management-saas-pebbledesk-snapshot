## PebbleDesk vs LifeCubby: Modern vs Incumbent

LifeCubby has a real customer base, particularly among home-based daycare providers who adopted it years ago and built workflows around it. The product covers the basics; attendance, daily reports, parent updates, and it has been stable enough to keep that customer base.

PebbleDesk was built for the same price tier with a different design priority: compliance-first features that the older generation of small-center tools never built. Real-time ratio alerts. State-formatted CCDF and DHS subsidy reports. One-click audit documentation. A modern interface designed to be used on a tablet during the workday in a bright classroom.

## The Feature Gap

LifeCubby tracks attendance. PebbleDesk tracks attendance, watches ratios in real time, and alerts the director the moment a room is at risk of going out of compliance. That is the difference between catching a problem and learning about it from a licensing inspector.

LifeCubby has billing features. PebbleDesk has billing features plus state-formatted subsidy reports for CCDF and DHS voucher reconciliation. For centers where 30-50% of families are subsidy-funded, that difference is hours per month and meaningful agency-payment accuracy.

LifeCubby produces reports. PebbleDesk produces audit-ready exports formatted for what state licensing inspectors actually ask for during a visit.

## When LifeCubby Still Makes Sense

If you have used LifeCubby for years, your staff is trained, your workflows are stable, and your operation does not run into compliance or subsidy pain; the case for switching is weak. Stable software that your team knows is genuinely valuable.

If any of those conditions are missing, particularly if subsidy billing or audit prep takes hours every cycle; the feature gap is large enough that the migration usually pays for itself quickly.
