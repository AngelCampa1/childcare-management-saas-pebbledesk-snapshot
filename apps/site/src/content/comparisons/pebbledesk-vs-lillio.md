## Lillio vs PebbleDesk: Learning vs Compliance

Lillio earned its reputation under the name HiMama as the platform that helped educators document children's learning. Daily reports, developmental milestones, photo portfolios; the parent-facing experience is one of the strongest in the category, and that has not changed since the 2023 rebrand.

PebbleDesk was built for a different problem. The product exists because directors at licensed centers spend hours every month reconciling CCDF voucher payments, monitoring classroom ratios manually, and pulling records together when state inspectors arrive. PebbleDesk handles those workflows directly: real-time ratio alerts, state-formatted subsidy billing reports, one-click audit documentation.

Both products are good at what they do. The trade-off is which problem hurts your center more right now.

## Picking Between Them

If your center markets itself on developmental quality and parents expect rich learning documentation, Lillio is the better fit. Switching to a compliance-first platform would cost you a feature parents have come to expect.

If your center's biggest monthly pains are subsidy billing reconciliation, ratio tracking, and audit prep, those problems are exactly what PebbleDesk is built to solve. The pricing math also matters: PebbleDesk Center Starter is {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}} for licensed centers, while Lillio requires current written terms before directors can compare annual cost.

A few centers run both systems during a transition. That works for a month or two but creates duplicate data entry that wears staff down quickly. Most directors end up picking one platform based on which problem is costing more time today.
