## Two Tools Solving Different Problems

Lillio and Procare are not really competing for the same buyer. They solved different problems and ended up being used together by centers that need both.

Lillio started as HiMama, focused entirely on parent engagement. Daily activity logs, developmental observations, and family-facing communication were the product. The billing and administrative features came later and remain secondary.

Procare started as billing software for childcare centers. Tuition invoicing, accounts receivable, and subsidy billing were the core. Parent communication was not in scope.

This historical divergence explains why so many centers end up paying for both.

## The Double-Tool Problem

A director using both Lillio and Procare manages two separate systems with no native integration. Attendance data lives in Lillio. Billing data lives in Procare. When a parent questions a charge, the director checks Procare. When a parent asks about their child's day, they check Lillio. When a state inspector asks for attendance records, those records are in Lillio. When a subsidy agency audits billing claims, those records are in Procare.

The data is scattered, and reconciling it; confirming that the children billed in Procare actually attended the days Lillio recorded; is a manual task.

Combined cost: Lillio plus Procare requires two written quotes before budgeting. That is before considering the time cost of managing two platforms.

## What Each Misses

Lillio misses: billing depth, subsidy reconciliation, and compliance documentation for state licensing visits. The curriculum and observation records are excellent; the attendance records formatted for a licensing inspector are not.

Procare misses: parent communication, modern interface, and mobile accessibility. The billing records are thorough; the experience for teachers using the system in a classroom is not designed for that use case.

Both miss: real-time ratio alerts. Neither platform flags when a classroom drops below licensed ratio in the moment it happens.
