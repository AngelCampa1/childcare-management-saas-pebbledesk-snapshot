## ChildPilot overview

ChildPilot covers the standard childcare management features: attendance tracking, check-in/check-out, billing, and parent communication. It handles basic operations for directors who need to digitize daily records.

The gaps show up in compliance automation. Ratio monitoring is passive (track attendance, calculate ratios manually) rather than proactive (alert staff when a room approaches its limit). Subsidy billing workflows are not designed for the complexity of CCAP reconciliation across multiple funding sources.

## Why compliance-first matters

Directors who have been through a surprise licensing audit know the difference between a platform that records data and one that keeps you compliant. Recording attendance is necessary but not sufficient. Proactive ratio monitoring, audit-ready report generation, and subsidy tracking are what prevent citations and reimbursement delays.
