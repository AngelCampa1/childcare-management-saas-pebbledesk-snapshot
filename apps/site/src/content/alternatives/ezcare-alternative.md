## What EZCare gets right

EZCare has been processing childcare billing for a long time. The platform handles tuition invoicing, subsidy co-pay billing, aging receivables, and multi-rate enrollment scenarios that simpler tools cannot. For a large childcare program that processes hundreds of billing transactions per month across complex rate structures, EZCare's accounts receivable depth is real.

Church daycares and larger programs that adopted EZCare a decade ago often have staff who know the system well. The billing module works. The reports are detailed. For an organization with dedicated administrative staff and an IT department, the complexity is manageable.

## Where EZCare falls short for modern licensed centers

The core problem is architecture. EZCare was built in the desktop software era. Running it cloud-native requires the SofterWare hosted version, which adds cost and does not fully solve the mobile access problem. Staff who need to take attendance from an iPad in the classroom find EZCare's interface frustrating.

Implementation is not self-service. Getting EZCare running properly typically requires professional services engagement, data mapping, and configuration work that takes weeks. For a director who wants to switch software before the next quarter, that timeline is a barrier.

Pricing is a black box. EZCare does not publish what it costs, which means every prospective buyer must go through a sales process before understanding whether the investment makes sense. Directors switching from EZCare frequently report they were paying significantly more than comparable modern platforms charge.

The compliance gap is significant for centers subject to licensing visits. EZCare does not provide real-time ratio alerts. When staff leave a classroom and coverage drops, EZCare will not tell you. When a licensing officer asks for a specific afternoon's ratio records, the export requires manual formatting. These are not edge cases; they happen regularly in licensed childcare operations.

## What the audit actually requires

State licensing inspectors ask for documentation that EZCare partially handles and partially does not:

- Tuition and subsidy billing records: EZCare handles this well
- Staff-to-child ratio records at specific times: EZCare does not generate these automatically
- Staff credential tracking and expiration alerts: outside EZCare's core scope
- Attendance records formatted for state agency submission: requires export and reformatting

PebbleDesk handles all four from a single platform.
