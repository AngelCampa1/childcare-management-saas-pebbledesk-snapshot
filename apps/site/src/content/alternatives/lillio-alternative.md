## Lillio's strengths

Lillio (formerly HiMama) has strong parent communication features. Daily reports, photos, and activity logs work well. The parent-facing experience is polished.

For Canadian childcare programs, Lillio's compliance features are more directly relevant. The platform was built for Canadian provincial documentation requirements, and reviews from Canadian users are consistently positive on the compliance side.

## The US compliance gap

US childcare directors operate in a different regulatory environment than Canadian programs. Common requirements include:

- Staff-to-child ratio documentation at specific intervals throughout the day
- Attendance records that match subsidy billing claims
- Incident reports in state-specified formats
- Subsidy reconciliation reports formatted for DHS, CCDF, or Head Start submission

Lillio generates documentation and reports, but the formats reflect Canadian regulatory conventions. US directors report that reconciling Lillio exports with what their state subsidy agency requires involves manual reformatting. The parent communication export is clean; the licensing documentation export requires additional work.

## Pricing opacity

Lillio does not publish pricing. Evaluating fit requires a sales call before you know whether the cost fits your budget.

Lillio's pricing scales with student count and user count. Directors at growing centers report costs increasing faster than expected as enrollment grows. The combined per-student and per-user structure makes the total hard to predict.

PebbleDesk pricing is public. Home is {{plan.home.priceLabel}}. Center Starter is {{plan.center_starter.priceLabel}}, and Center Pro is {{plan.center_pro.priceLabel}}. No sales call required, no per-child variable, no setup fee.

## App performance

Lillio users report app slowness when working with video content. Slow uploads during a busy morning drop-off interrupt workflow.

PebbleDesk does not support video upload. Documentation is text-based: attendance check-ins, ratio snapshots, incident reports, medication logs, and subsidy records. Text records load quickly on any device and are easier to search, export, and present to a licensing officer than a video library.

That is a deliberate tradeoff. Lillio is the better tool if your primary need is parent engagement through rich media. PebbleDesk is the better tool if your primary need is compliance documentation that holds up in an audit.

## Support scaling problems

Directors report that Lillio support has slowed as the company has grown. For a director dealing with a billing discrepancy the week before a licensing inspection, response time is not an abstract concern.

We are a smaller operation and do not have the same volume pressures. We commit to email support with a response within one business day.
