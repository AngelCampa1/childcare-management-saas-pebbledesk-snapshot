## The Kangarootime Approach

Kangarootime is designed specifically for licensed childcare operations, which differentiates it from consumer-facing platforms like Brightwheel. The per-class pricing ($8/classroom/month) makes costs predictable for multi-room centers. Staff scheduling is more developed than most competitors.

The gaps are in compliance automation. Ratio tracking is attendance-based, not proactive. The system logs who is checked in but does not alert staff when a room is approaching its ratio limit. Subsidy billing features exist but require manual reconciliation for CCAP and voucher-funded families.

## Where Kangarootime Falls Short for Compliance-Focused Directors

Directors whose primary concern is audit readiness need two things from their software: proactive ratio monitoring and audit-ready report generation. Kangarootime provides the data inputs (attendance, check-in/check-out) but leaves the compliance layer to the director.
