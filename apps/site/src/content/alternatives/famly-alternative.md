## Famly: a European platform in the US market

Famly was founded in Denmark and built for the European childcare market. The platform is well-designed, has a clean mobile app, and covers center management well. It has been expanding into the US market.

The challenge for US center directors is that compliance requirements are state-specific. Each state has its own ratio requirements, licensing documentation formats, and subsidy billing processes. A platform designed for European regulatory frameworks may not cover the specifics of a California CCLD audit or a Texas DFPS inspection.
