## What this enrollment forms packet includes

These free childcare enrollment forms combine the paperwork most centers collect before a child's first day: a daycare registration form template, enrollment agreement, emergency contact form, authorized pickup form, health and immunization record prompt, emergency medical authorization, photo release, tuition terms, and parent handbook acknowledgment.

Use this packet when you need free printable daycare enrollment forms that are more complete than a one-page contact sheet. It is also a practical starting point if families are searching for a childcare enrollment forms pdf, a daycare enrollment form template, or a daycare registration form template they can adapt before enrollment.

The packet is organized so each section answers a specific director question:

- Who is the child, who are the guardians, and what classroom or schedule is being requested?
- Who can be contacted in an emergency, and who is authorized for pickup?
- What health, immunization, allergy, medication, and emergency treatment information must be on file?
- What tuition, fee, attendance, illness, late pickup, withdrawal, and termination rules has the family accepted?
- Which releases and acknowledgments did the parent or guardian sign?

## Before you use these forms

Fill in every bracketed field before presenting the packet to families. Do not leave brackets in the final version because they indicate required customization.

These forms are not state-specific legal advice, a state-approved enrollment packet, or a replacement for forms required by licensing, subsidy, food program, medication, transportation, or health departments. Compare the packet against your current licensing checklist, then have your attorney review payment terms, termination clauses, releases, and liability language before using the forms with families.

Keep the signed packet in the child's file, give the family a copy, and review the information annually. Contact details, authorized pickup adults, immunization records, allergies, and billing terms change often enough that one-time enrollment paperwork becomes stale quickly.

## Childcare enrollment form

Start the packet with the facts staff need before the first day of care. This section works as the childcare enrollment form and registration form: child identity, guardian contacts, classroom assignment, schedule, emergency contact, authorized pickup, health notes, and required records.

## Childcare enrollment agreement

After the registration information is complete, use the agreement section to document the financial and operational terms the family accepts. This is where tuition, payment due dates, late fees, attendance expectations, illness exclusions, withdrawal notice, and signatures belong.

### Late Pickup Policy

[CENTER NAME] closes at [CLOSING TIME]. Children must be signed out by an authorized adult by this time.

A late pickup fee of $[AMOUNT] per minute is assessed for any pickup after [CLOSING TIME]. This fee covers the cost of staff required to remain beyond their scheduled shift. Late fees are added to your account and are due with your next tuition payment.

**Repeated late pickup (three or more occurrences in a calendar month) may result in enrollment termination.**

If a child has not been picked up by [CLOSING TIME + 15-30 minutes] and [CENTER NAME] cannot reach any authorized adult, [CENTER NAME] will follow its written late-pickup escalation procedure, including required licensing, emergency, or local-agency notifications.

### Illness Exclusion Policy

Children must be kept home when they exhibit any of the following symptoms:

- Fever of [100.4°F / 101°F or higher]; child may not return until fever-free for 24 hours without fever-reducing medication
- Vomiting; child may not return until 24 hours symptom-free
- Diarrhea (two or more episodes in a 24-hour period); child may not return until 24 hours symptom-free
- Contagious or undiagnosed rash
- Pink eye (conjunctivitis); child may not return until 24 hours after beginning treatment
- Strep throat; child may not return until 24 hours after beginning antibiotic treatment and fever-free
- Lice; child may not return until all live lice and nits have been treated and removed
- Any illness that prevents the child from participating comfortably in activities

*If a child develops symptoms during the care day, the parent/guardian will be contacted for immediate pickup. Please ensure [CENTER NAME] has a current phone number where you can be reached during care hours.*
