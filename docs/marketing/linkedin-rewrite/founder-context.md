# PebbleDesk Founder Context And Truth Rules

PebbleDesk does not have clients yet.

The product was built by an engineer/founder who saw a gap in the childcare administration market: licensed childcare operators have high-stakes attendance, ratio, subsidy, billing, records, and audit work, but much of that work is still split across paper, spreadsheets, portals, and software that does not keep the operating record connected.

## What Content Can Truthfully Say

- PebbleDesk is built for licensed childcare centers, family childcare homes, and multi-site childcare operators.
- PebbleDesk was built around audit-ready records, ratio coverage, subsidy paperwork, attendance, billing, and reporting.
- PebbleDesk's point of view is based on market research, public childcare compliance/billing requirements, and the founder's product judgment.
- PebbleDesk supports the product capabilities described in the current site config and feature pages.

## What Content Must Not Say

- Do not imply PebbleDesk has customers, clients, users, case studies, testimonials, logos, reviews, or adoption data.
- Do not say "our customers", "clients tell us", "directors using PebbleDesk", "teams using PebbleDesk", or "centers on PebbleDesk".
- Do not imply customer outcomes such as "customers save hours", "centers reduced errors", or "operators switched and saw results".
- Do not describe a customer story unless it is clearly hypothetical or based on public/repo source material outside PebbleDesk usage.
- Do not use social-proof language like "trusted by", "used by", "loved by", or "proven by customers".

## Safe Framing

Prefer:

- "PebbleDesk was built because..."
- "The product thesis is..."
- "The market gap is..."
- "A practical test for directors is..."
- "The repo/source guide notes..."
- "Public/program data cited in the guide shows..."

Avoid:

- "We built this after hearing from customers..."
- "Directors using PebbleDesk..."
- "Our users..."
- "Centers have found..."
- "Teams save..."
