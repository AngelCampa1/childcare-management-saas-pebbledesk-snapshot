## What Worked

The strongest posts were concrete operating moments, not abstract product language:

- Enrollment records as live operating proof: top post, 362 impressions and 2 clicks.
- Ratio risk during room moves, pickup, breaks, and transition windows: two top-five posts.
- Attendance and billing proof: practical finance and family-balance questions performed above baseline.
- Emergency readiness: binder access and evacuation-ready records performed well.
- Staff credential and CACFP posts created the few comment/reaction signals in the export.

## What To Do Next

The next calendar keeps the 15-post daily cadence but shifts the center of gravity toward direct director problems:

- More posts that start with a specific room, record, family, staff, billing, or licensing moment.
- Fewer posts that explain PebbleDesk philosophy in the abstract.
- More buyer tests a director can run during a demo, trial, audit prep, or weekly closeout.
- More source-backed operational education across enrollment, ratios, attendance, billing, subsidy, CACFP, emergency readiness, multi-site reporting, and waitlist operations.
- Honest product boundaries where needed, stated in buyer language rather than internal scope language.
