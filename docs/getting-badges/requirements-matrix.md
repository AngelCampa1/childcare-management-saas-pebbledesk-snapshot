## SaaSHub

Submission URL: https://www.saashub.com/services/submit

Sources:

- Submit page: https://www.saashub.com/services/submit

Verified requirements:

- Add a product website URL or product page URL.
- Add relevant categories.
- List competitors. SaaSHub says submissions without competitors are slowed down
  and pushed to the bottom of the queue.
- Verify the product with an email address on the product domain for higher priority.
- Product must be released, in English, and not hosted on a free subdomain.
- SaaS, IaaS, PaaS, software products, apps with decent websites, and niche-leading
  websites are acceptable.

PebbleDesk fit:

- Good fit. PebbleDesk is a released SaaS product on `pebbledesk.app` with a public
  website, pricing, terms, privacy, signup, and comparison content.

Fields to prepare:

- Website: `https://pebbledesk.app`
- Product name: `PebbleDesk`
- Categories: `Education`, `School Management`, `Workforce Management`, `Billing`,
  `Document Management`, `Productivity`
- Competitors: Brightwheel, Procare, Lillio, Playground, Kangarootime, Famly, iCare,
  Jackrabbit Care, Sawyer, Xplor
- Short description: use the SaaSHub short copy in `submission-copy.md`.
- Long description: use the SaaSHub long copy in `submission-copy.md`.
- Verification email: use an address on `pebbledesk.app`.

Acceptance risks:

- Avoid positioning it as a waiting-list product. Submit the working product URL.
- Keep categories relevant. SaaSHub accepts broad software categories, but childcare
  administration is a niche under education and operations.

Source notes:

- SaaSHub requests categories, competitors, domain verification, and product URL.
- SaaSHub rejects unreleased products, free-subdomain products, non-English products,
  agencies, and waiting-list landing pages.

## AlternativeTo

Submission URL: https://alternativeto.net/

Sources:

- Public browse taxonomy: https://alternativeto.net/browse/
- Public app listing examples: https://alternativeto.net/

Publicly verified listing fields:

- AlternativeTo is a crowd-sourced app and software alternatives directory.
- Public app pages expose license, platform, application type, screenshots, short
  description, features, tags, official website, and alternatives.
- Browse taxonomy includes categories, licenses, features, tags, and platforms.
- Public pages show web and SaaS platforms, paid and freemium licenses, and
  competitor alternatives as core listing elements.

PebbleDesk fit:

- Good fit if submitted as an online SaaS app in business, education, and home/family
  software categories.
- Strongest listing angle is "alternative to Brightwheel, Procare, Lillio, Playground,
  and Kangarootime for audit-ready childcare administration."

Fields to prepare before opening the logged-in form:

- Product name: `PebbleDesk`
- Official website: `https://pebbledesk.app`
- Short description: use the AlternativeTo short copy in `submission-copy.md`.
- License default: choose `Paid with free trial` if the form offers it. Otherwise
  choose `Paid`.
- Platforms: `Online`, `Software as a Service (SaaS)`
- Application type: childcare management software, school management software, billing
  software, attendance tracking software
- Features/tags: attendance tracking, ratio tracking, billing, audit reports, staff
  scheduling, subsidy billing, childcare management, online
- Alternatives to add: Brightwheel, Procare, Lillio, Playground, Kangarootime, Famly
- Screenshots: at least 3 clean product screenshots with no sensitive data.

Acceptance risks:

- The full add-app form was not publicly visible without login during this pass.
  Recheck the logged-in form before submitting.
- Treat AlternativeTo as form-check blocked until the logged-in flow confirms the
  accepted field names, upload requirements, taxonomy values, and destination path.
- AlternativeTo users dislike inflated claims. Keep the description plain and
  alternative-oriented.

Source notes:

- AlternativeTo public pages verify category, license, tag, feature, and platform
  taxonomy, but they do not verify every required field in the logged-in submission
  form.
- Public app entries verify that listings show screenshots, license, platforms, app
  type, official website, and alternatives.

## Product Hunt

Submission URL: https://www.producthunt.com/launch

Sources:

- Launch guide: https://www.producthunt.com/launch
- Preparation checklist: https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch

Verified requirements:

- Submit with a personal Product Hunt account, not a company account.
- Product URL must be direct and cannot be a shortened link or UTM tracking link.
- Product name should be only the name, with no description or emojis.
- Tagline maximum is 60 characters.
- Description maximum is 500 characters.
- Choose up to 3 launch tags.
- Thumbnail is required, square, recommended 240x240, under 3 MB.
- Gallery requires at least 2 images before the post page is viewable; recommended
  gallery image size is 1270x760.
- Optional video must be a full YouTube URL, not private, and not shortened.
- Add maker usernames before launch.
- Add a first comment. Product Hunt states 70% of products that achieved Product of
  the Day, Week, or Month included a first comment by the maker.
- Product Hunt allows launches to be scheduled up to 1 month in advance.
- Product Hunt says 12:01 AM Pacific Time is the best launch time for makers planning
  ahead, but the best day is the day the team is most prepared.
- Product Hunt says not to ask directly for upvotes. Ask people to visit and comment.

PebbleDesk fit:

- Moderate fit. Product Hunt can support credibility, a backlink, early feedback, and
  founder story, but childcare operators are not the core Product Hunt audience.
- Submit only when the gallery explains the product in seconds and the founder can
  reply to comments quickly.

Fields to prepare:

- URL: `https://pebbledesk.app`
- Name: `PebbleDesk`
- Tagline: use the Product Hunt tagline in `submission-copy.md`.
- Description: use the Product Hunt description in `submission-copy.md`.
- Tags: use `Productivity`, `Education`, and `Finance` as the default picks. If one
  is not available in the current launch-tag menu, pause and record the closest
  available replacement in the tracker before submitting.
- Pricing: paid with free trial.
- Makers: Angel Campa plus any real co-maker accounts.
- First comment: use the Product Hunt maker comment in `submission-copy.md`.
- Shoutouts: only list tools that genuinely helped build the product.

Acceptance risks:

- Product Hunt audience may not include many childcare buyers. Use it for credibility
  and feedback rather than expecting direct buyer volume.
- A weak gallery will bury the product. Do not launch without real product visuals.
- Never ask for upvotes.

## G2

Submission entry point: https://documentation.g2.com/help/en

Do not use `https://www.g2.com/products/new`; it returned `410 Gone` during the
second source review. G2's public help path now routes product-add guidance through
the G2 help center and the "add it here" flow.

Sources:

- Reviewer help center: https://documentation.g2.com/help/en
- Research FAQ: https://research.g2.com/methodology/research-faq
- Product information docs: https://documentation.g2.com/g2/docs/product-information
- Screenshot docs: https://documentation.g2.com/docs/screenshots
- Badge docs: https://documentation.g2.com/docs/g2-badges

Verified requirements:

- Check whether the product or company is already listed under another name before
  submitting a new profile.
- Software companies should have one G2 listing per product.
- G2 lists B2B software and services; B2C products are rejected.
- Duplicate listings are rejected.
- Integrations or plugins built on an existing product are usually not eligible as
  separate listings.
- G2 can reject if the website is inaccessible during review.
- G2 category eligibility is based on functionality and category inclusion criteria.
- Product profile names must match the actual product name and should not include
  marketing language.
- Profile logo must be at least 400 px and JPG, PNG, or GIF.
- Grid logo must be SVG under 5 MB. G2 recommends image-based, not text-based, grid logos.
- Profile banner supports JPG, PNG, or GIF under 5 MB. Recommended dimensions are
  1260x240, or 2500x476 if rendering needs more resolution.
- Product overview can be up to 500 words.
- Screenshot gallery allows up to 6 JPG, PNG, or GIF images under 5 MB each.
- Screenshots must not include proprietary details or sensitive customer information.
- Video banner supports Vimeo, Vidyard, Wistia, and YouTube Universal Links.

PebbleDesk fit:

- Good category fit as B2B childcare center administration software.
- Submit only if the product should be represented as generally available. G2 says
  alpha or beta products are not accepted in its add-product flow.

Fields to prepare:

- Product name: `PebbleDesk`
- Seller name: `PebbleDesk`
- Website: `https://pebbledesk.app`
- Primary category request: `Childcare Management Software`
- Backup category language: if G2 does not expose that exact category, request the
  closest education operations category and state that PebbleDesk is B2B software for
  licensed childcare centers, not a consumer parent app.
- Software type: Hosted in the cloud.
- Languages supported: English.
- Product overview: use the G2 profile copy in `submission-copy.md`.
- Packages and pricing: use the shared pricing source in
  `packages/shared/src/constants/billing.ts` and the generated public file at
  `apps/site/public/pricing.md`. Use that file for trial and no-credit-card
  requirement, and current limited-offer public pricing only if the form has room
  for offer detail.
- Assets: profile logo, grid SVG logo, banner, and up to 6 product screenshots.

Acceptance risks:

- If PebbleDesk is described as beta anywhere in the submitted materials, G2 can reject.
- Category fit may require public feature evidence. Link to pricing and feature pages
  that show attendance, ratio tracking, subsidy workflows, billing, reports, imports,
  and multi-location support where applicable.
- Meaningful G2 badge value requires verified reviews after the listing is live.
- G2 documentation says Users Love Us is earned after 20 reviews with a 4.0+ average
  rating, while Grid badges depend on market report placement.

Source notes:

- G2 profile, screenshot, category, and badge requirements were checked against G2
  documentation and research FAQ pages.
- G2 badge requirement reference: https://documentation.g2.com/docs/g2-badges

## BetaList

Submission URL: https://betalist.com/submit

Sources:

- Criteria: https://betalist.com/criteria
- Submission terms: https://betalist.com/terms/submissions
- Support FAQ: https://betalist.com/support

Verified requirements:

- Product should be recently launched or still unreleased.
- Product should not already have significant press coverage.
- Each startup can be featured twice: once pre-launch and once during launch, with at
  least a few weeks between posts.
- Product must be a technology startup, not a blog, course, book, newsletter,
  subscription ecommerce product, consultancy, or agency.
- Product needs a distinct, decent-looking landing page with enough product information.
- BetaList rejects free hosting subdomains and direct app-store-only links.
- Visitors must be able to sign up, download, log in, or otherwise get access.
- Submitter must be authorized to represent the startup.
- Startup must have a working website on its own domain.
- Submission does not guarantee featuring.
- Priority listing can speed review, but it does not guarantee acceptance.
- Standard review can take up to a few weeks. Priority review can happen in a few days.

PebbleDesk fit:

- Conditional fit. PebbleDesk is a technology startup with a product website and
  signup path, but BetaList is strongest when the product is pre-launch or recently
  launched.

Fields to prepare:

- Startup name: `PebbleDesk`
- Website: `https://pebbledesk.app`
- One-line pitch: use the BetaList one-liner in `submission-copy.md`.
- Description: use the BetaList description in `submission-copy.md`.
- Founder: Angel Campa
- Primary category: `SaaS`
- Suggested tags or secondary descriptors, if available: education, childcare,
  operations, compliance
- Signup path: homepage or pricing page with free-trial CTA.
- Screenshot: use clean product or landing-page imagery after assets are exported.

Acceptance risks:

- If PebbleDesk has been public for weeks or longer, BetaList may be a poor fit.
- If the page reads like mature marketing rather than an early launch, the submission
  may be less interesting to the BetaList audience.
- Priority listing is optional and should not be treated as guaranteed placement.
- Use the standard submission flow first. If BetaList offers priority details after
  intake, pay only if the timing is worth a faster review.
